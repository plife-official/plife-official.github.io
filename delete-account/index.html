방법 1. 앱에서 직접 삭제

앱이 설치되어 있고 로그인할 수 있다면 아래 경로에서 직접 삭제하실 수 있습니다. 별도 요청 없이 즉시 처리됩니다.

  1. 풀라이프 앱 실행 후 하단 설정 탭 이동
  2. 계정 항목의 회원 탈퇴 선택
  3. 안내를 확인하고 탈퇴 → 최종 확인에서 다시 탈퇴 선택

This is the official page for requesting deletion of your plife account and all associated data.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

Open the plife app → Settings tab → Delete account → confirm twice. Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email plife.kr@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”, stating your sign-in method (Kakao or Apple) and your sign-up email or nickname. We verify and complete the deletion within 7 business days. No app reinstall is required.

What is deleted

Account credentials, email, nickname and profile image; all registered plants and watering settings; all diary entries and notes; all watering history; all uploaded photos; and the push notification token. Everything is permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

What is retained

No personal data is retained after deletion, and no statutory retention obligation applies. Only anonymous, non-identifiable statistics and crash logs remain, solely for service quality purposes.
